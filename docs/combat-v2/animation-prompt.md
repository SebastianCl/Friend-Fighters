# Generación de animaciones

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. Referencias: sprite y retrato aprobados de visual-v1.

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production fighting-game animation spritesheet, one transparent PNG, 2048x2048 square.
Image 1 is the APPROVED fighter full-body sprite. Image 2 is her approved portrait. Preserve exactly her illustrated identity, face, long dark hair, warm skin tone, body proportions, dark loose T-shirt, beige pleated skirt, black fingerless gloves with red wraps, brown lace-up boots. Crisp detailed pixel art with dark stepped outlines and block shading. No generic anime redesign.
Create a STRICT 4 COLUMN by 4 ROW contact sheet, SIXTEEN separate full-body animation poses, read left to right, top to bottom. Each cell is exactly one quarter of image width and height, all equal, no gaps between cells. Genuine transparent alpha background. NO gridlines, NO labels, NO numbers, NO typography, NO scenery, NO cast shadows. Never overlap neighboring cells; include transparent padding around each pose. All sixteen must be present.
Each cell has a consistent baseline at 92% of its height; the same anatomical scale throughout; standing fighter about 82% of cell height. Torso center at 45% cell width, looking/facing RIGHT. All hands, hair and boots fully contained, even extended attacks. Feet connect to this same baseline, except tucked jump knees. Preserve skirt modestly over hips during all movements.
EXACT ROWS:
Row 1: (1) walking guard, left foot leading; (2) walking guard, right foot leading; (3) airborne rising jump, knees bent, fists up; (4) airborne falling jump, legs extending to land.
Row 2: (5) low crouching guard, both knees bent, height 58% of standing; (6) standing block, both forearms shielding face; (7) hit reaction, head recoils, shoulders tilted, balanced feet; (8) knocked down lying horizontally on her back, head to LEFT, boots to RIGHT, on baseline.
Row 3: (9) punch anticipation, fist pulled back; (10) straight punch fully extended to RIGHT, shoulder and hip engaged; (11) kick anticipation, raised knee; (12) side kick fully extended to RIGHT at waist height, supporting foot on baseline, balanced torso.
Row 4: (13) crouching straight punch to RIGHT, low stance; (14) low sweeping kick to RIGHT, crouched torso; (15) special anticipation, both gloved hands gathered near chest; (16) special contact, both palms pushed forcefully to RIGHT, NO drawn energy effects (engine adds effects).
Use natural readable silhouettes with distinct limb articulation, NOT copies of the same guard pose. Same character size, face and clothes in every cell. Face detail matters.
```
